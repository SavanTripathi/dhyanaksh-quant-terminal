## 1. TIMEFRAME ISOLATION & CHART COORDINATE PARITY

- **Strict Timeframe Isolation:**
  - Near WDZ $\rightarrow$ Weekly ONLY
  - Near MDZ $\rightarrow$ Monthly ONLY
  - Near QDZ $\rightarrow$ Quarterly ONLY
  - Near DDZ $\rightarrow$ Daily ONLY
  - ATZ $\rightarrow \text{QDZ} \land \text{MDZ} \land \text{WDZ} \land \text{DDZ}$
- **Coordinate Integrity:** Chart geometry coordinates match exact underlying aggregated candle boundaries without spatial distortion.
- **Ledger Categorization in UI:** Prospective, Replay, and Invalidation states render with distinct badges and isolated statistics.
